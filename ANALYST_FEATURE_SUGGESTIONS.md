# Suggested Extra Features for Analyst Role

Here are additional features that would enhance the Analyst experience in NagarDrishti AI:

---

## 1. **Compare Areas**
- Side-by-side comparison of two or more areas for the same metrics
- Useful for identifying which area needs more attention
- Show traffic, AQI, energy, water in a comparison table or chart

## 2. **Historical Trends**
- View data trends over time (last 7 days, 30 days, 3 months)
- Line charts showing how metrics have changed
- Helps spot patterns (e.g. traffic worsening on weekends)

## 3. **Export to CSV/Excel**
- Export metric data, chart data, or report data to CSV/Excel
- For analysts to use in external tools (Excel, R, Python)
- Include filters: city, area, date range, metrics

## 4. **Custom Dashboards**
- Allow analysts to create saved dashboard layouts
- Pin favorite metrics or charts
- Rearrange cards and choose which metrics to display

## 5. **Anomaly Highlights**
- Auto-flag unusual values (e.g. AQI spike, traffic > 80%)
- Visual indicators (badges, colors) on metric cards
- "View anomalies" filter on City Data page

## 6. **Data Filters & Date Range**
- Filter by date/time range on all views
- Compare weekday vs weekend patterns
- Time-of-day sliders for focused analysis

## 7. **Saved Views / Bookmarks**
- Save frequently used filters (e.g. "Sitabuldi + Traffic + AQI")
- Quick access from a dropdown or sidebar
- Share view links with colleagues

## 8. **Comments & Annotations**
- Add notes or annotations to charts or specific data points
- Tag insights for later reference or sharing
- Optional: attach to PDF reports

## 9. **Benchmarking**
- Compare current metrics to city average or past period
- Show "Above/Below average" for each metric
- Target-setting (e.g. "Reduce traffic to < 60%")

## 10. **Correlation View**
- Show relationships between metrics (e.g. traffic vs pollution)
- Simple correlation matrix or scatter plots
- Helps identify cause–effect patterns

---

## Quick Wins (Easier to Implement)
- Export to CSV
- Date range filter
- Compare two areas (simple table)
- Anomaly badges on metric cards

## Medium Effort
- Historical trend charts
- Saved views/bookmarks
- Benchmarking vs city average

## Higher Effort
- Custom dashboards
- Correlation/cross-metric analysis
- Comments and annotations
